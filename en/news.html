Director's Insight

Standards and record of judgment

Principles for reading China, the judgment record, and selected books.

Judgment record · public scorecard

  1. As-of date
  2. Judgment
  3. Evidence
  4. Invalidation conditions
  5. Follow-up review

Each judgment is logged with its date, its evidence and the conditions that would prove it wrong, then reviewed later. Scenarios will carry probabilities.

As-of date 15 November 2026

Judgment The surge in Korea's exports to China in early November was driven by chip prices, not by a recovery in Chinese domestic demand.

Evidence In 1–10 November, chip export growth (331.2%) far exceeded growth in exports to China (148%).

Invalidation conditions We withdraw this judgment if the monthly data on 1 December show exports to China excluding chips growing faster than total exports excluding chips.

Follow-up review After the data release on 1 December 2026 · Read the note
