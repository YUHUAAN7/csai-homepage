Monthly China Economy Focus

Every second Tuesday morning: a month of China's macro economy, industries and supply chains in one issue, each article ending with what it means for Korea.

Macro

Growth, prices, total social financing, monetary and fiscal policy

Industry

New energy, semiconductors, platforms, property

Supply chains

Korea–China trade, critical materials, export controls
