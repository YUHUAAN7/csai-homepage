BUSINESS ADVISORY

Business Advisory & Consulting

We back the strategic decisions companies face amid shifts in China's policy, markets and supply chains with macro, industry and policy analysis.

Service AreasProcessCase ThemesInquiry
SERVICE AREAS

Service areas

Scope and format are agreed in consultation. CSAI does not recommend securities, manage assets or broker investments.

China exposure review

How exposed revenue, sourcing and investment are to China factors, set out in a review report.

Executive advisory sessions

Working through management's China call, one decision at a time.

Policy & regulatory impact

What changes in China's policy and regulation mean for the business.

Supply-chain review

Sourcing routes for key materials and parts, and export-control variables.

Market strategy review

Testing business strategy, including entry and exit, against trends in China's markets and industries.

Cross-border M&A strategy

Strategic advice only; we do not broker or arrange transactions.

In scope

  • Macro, industry, policy and supply-chain analysis
  • Support for companies' strategic decisions
  • Advice based on public sources and lawful research

Out of scope

  • Stock picks, price targets, trading strategies
  • Asset management, discretionary investment, brokerage
  • Brokering or arranging M&A transactions
  • Due-diligence or expert-network style investigation, or collecting non-public data
PROCESS

Process

Advisory is done one decision at a time. It starts with a review, moves through an eight-week paid pilot, and continues as an annual contract.

  1. China exposure reviewReview report
  2. Executive advisory sessionOne decision at a time
  3. Eight-week paid pilotA period to fit scope and format
  4. Annual advisory contractAdvisory continued on an annual basis
CASE THEMES

Case themes

The questions companies most often face, and advisory work we have carried out.

Typical questions

  • What a change in China's policy means for our industry
  • Items in our supply chain that depend heavily on China, and the alternatives
  • Risks and opportunities in the China market

Advisory work we have done

  • Advising Korea's largest food group on its investment in China
  • Advising an industry-leading Chinese company on investing and raising capital in Korea
  • Global market analysis for a Korean shipping company
  • Consulting for a major Korean conglomerate on winding down its China subsidiary

Client names are not disclosed.
